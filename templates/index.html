How Campus Lost & Found Works

Designed specifically for the fast-paced college environment to ensure no item remains misplaced for long.

01

Report or Search

Submit a lost report with photos, location, and date, or report an item you discovered on campus so the owner can spot it immediately.

02

Claim & Verify Ownership

Found something that belongs to you? Submit a claim with unique proof of ownership such as serial numbers, screen wallpapers, or receipts.

03

Admin Review & Return

Campus security or the finder verifies ownership and approves the handover. Collect your item securely at the Campus Security Station.
